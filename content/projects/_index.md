### Publications

## 2026

- [A Tensor-Train Multidimensional Inverse Laplace Transform](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06093)
  <span class="entry-desc">Inverts multidimensional Laplace transforms in tensor-train format, cutting the cost from exponential to polynomial in the number of dimensions.</span>


## 2025

- [Fast and Flexible Quantum-Inspired PDE Solvers with Data Integration](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.17046)
  <span class="entry-desc">Solves linear and nonlinear PDEs in quantized tensor-train format with logarithmic memory and runtime, and adds a data-driven learning scheme.</span>

## 2022
- [Threshold photoproduction of neutral pions off protons in nuclear model with explicit mesons](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00601-022-01783-9)
  <span class="entry-desc">Computes near-threshold cross sections for neutral pion photoproduction in a model where nucleons emit and absorb mesons explicitly rather than interacting through a potential.</span>

### Code

- [FewSpecialFunctions.jl](https://github.com/MartinMikkelsen/FewSpecialFunctions.jl)
  <span class="entry-desc">Julia implementations of Coulomb wave, Clausen, Debye, Fresnel, parabolic cylinder, and Marcum-Q functions, plus Fermi-Dirac integrals and the Voigt profile.</span>
- [FewBodyPhysics.jl](https://github.com/MartinMikkelsen/FewBodyPhysics.jl)
  <span class="entry-desc">Variational solver for quantum few-body systems using explicitly correlated Gaussian basis functions.</span>
- [TensorTrainNumerics.jl](https://github.com/MartinMikkelsen/TensorTrainNumerics.jl)
  <span class="entry-desc">Numerical methods in tensor-train and quantics tensor-train format: linear solvers, eigenvalue problems, time evolution, and TT-cross approximation.</span>
